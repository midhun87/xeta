How It Works

Your End-to-End Hiring Workflow

We automate every step, from the initial job post to the final offer, ensuring a seamless and secure process.

1. Job Posting

Post jobs and target specific colleges or open applications.

2. AI Screening

Auto-verify documents and score resumes against the job description.

3. Test Assignment

Assign coding, aptitude, or MCQ tests to shortlisted candidates.

4. Proctoring

Candidates take tests in the secure, AI-monitored Testify client.

5. Interview

Auto-schedule and conduct in-person or online interviews.

6. Selection

Analyze feedback and analytics to make the final hiring decision.
