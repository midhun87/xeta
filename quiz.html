📖 How to Play

  • Click "Start Game" to begin the memorization phase
  • You will have 2 minutes to memorize all 25 questions
  • All cards will be shown face-up during the memorization phase
  • After 2 minutes, all cards will automatically flip face-down
  • You will then have 15 minutes to answer all questions
  • In the answer phase, select a question number (1-25) and type your answer from memory
  • Press Enter in the question number field to select, or press Enter in the answer field to submit
  • Correct answers will reveal the card in green
  • Incorrect answers keep the card hidden - try again!
  • Complete all 25 cards to win the game!
