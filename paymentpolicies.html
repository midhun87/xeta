Xeta Tech Solutions Payment Policies

Please read the Xeta Tech Solutions billing, cancellation, and refund policies carefully before completing your transaction.

1. Registration & Payment

All transactions and registrations processed through Xeta Tech Solutions are considered final upon successful payment verification. By making a payment, you agree to these terms and conditions. Ensure that all details are accurate before proceeding to checkout.

2. No Cancellation by User

Once a payment is successfully completed, no cancellations or refunds will be entertained under any circumstances.

This includes, but is not limited to, change of mind, medical emergencies, scheduling conflicts, or failure to attend an event (no-shows). Tickets or service payments are generally non-transferable unless explicitly stated otherwise by the organizing committee or administrators.

3. Cancellation by Admin / Organizer

Refunds will only be initiated if the event or service is officially cancelled by the Admins or Organizers. In such an unlikely scenario, the base transaction amount will be automatically refunded to the original payment method within 7-10 business days.

4. Platform & Gateway Fees

Please note: At any cost, the platform fee and payment gateway charges are strictly non-refundable.

Even in the event of an official cancellation by the organizers where the base ticket price is refunded, the platform and processing fees incurred during the transaction will not be given back. These fees are utilized instantly by third-party processors.

5. Disputes & Support

If you face a payment failure where the amount was deducted from your bank but the confirmation was not generated, please do not attempt a second payment immediately. Contact our support team with your transaction ID.
