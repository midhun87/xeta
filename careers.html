Our Hiring Process

We want your application experience to be clear, fair, and secure. Please take note of our official process.

  • Official Portal: All subsequent rounds and communications will be conducted exclusively through the Testify Portal.
  • No Fees Guaranteed: We will never charge any single rupee or fee at any stage of the hiring process.
