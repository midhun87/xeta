Frontend Developer (React)

In-Person

Seeking a React developer to build and optimize our user-facing platforms. You'll work with modern frontend technologies, translating UI/UX designs into responsive, high-performance web applications.

  • 0-2 years of React.js experience
  • Strong in HTML, CSS, JavaScript (ES6+)
  • Experience with Tailwind CSS is a plus
  • Familiar with REST APIs

Backend Developer (Node.js)

In-Person

Join our backend team to design, build, and maintain scalable, secure, and high-performance server-side applications using Node.js, Express, and modern database technologies.

  • 1-2 years of Node.js & Express experience
  • Proficient with MongoDB or PostgreSQL
  • Strong understanding of REST APIs & microservices
  • Experience with Docker and CI/CD

Full Stack Developer (MERN)

In-Person

We're looking for a versatile MERN stack developer to work on both frontend and backend components. You'll be responsible for the entire development lifecycle, from concept to deployment.

  • 1-2 years of MERN (MongoDB, Express, React, Node) stack experience
  • Strong problem-solving skills
  • Ability to work independently
  • Experience with Git and agile methodologies

Full Stack Developer (MEAN)

In-Person

Are you an expert in the MEAN stack? We need a developer to manage and build complex applications using MongoDB, Express, Angular, and Node.js, focusing on scalability and robust architecture.

  • 1-2 years of MEAN (MongoDB, Express, Angular, Node) stack experience
  • Proficient in TypeScript
  • Strong understanding of component-based architecture
  • Experience with RxJS

Cloud Engineer (AWS)

In-Person

Seeking an experienced AWS Cloud Engineer to manage our cloud infrastructure. You will be responsible for deployment, automation, monitoring, and ensuring the security and scalability of our services.

  • 0-2 years of experience with AWS services (EC2, S3, RDS, Lambda)
  • Strong knowledge of CI/CD pipelines
  • Experience with Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, CloudFormation)
  • AWS Certification is a strong plus
